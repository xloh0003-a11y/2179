Malaysia's Transit Network at a Glance

Malaysia's public transit pivot is still unfolding. Rail ridership has recovered strongly from pandemic lows, but the network's reach remains concentrated in Greater Kuala Lumpur, leaving commuters in outer states heavily dependent on private vehicles. LRT and MRT lines lead in volume and growth velocity, while bus networks serve as the connective tissue binding areas that rail has yet to reach. Understanding how these systems perform, where passengers move, and when they travel is essential to mapping the equity gaps that remain.
